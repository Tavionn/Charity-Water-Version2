Flow Quest

Two sides of one mission: getting clean water to the people who need it. Choose your role.

Move ←→ or AD · Jump ↑ or Space · Interact E · Pause P

Use the buttons below: ← → to move, ↑ to jump, and the hand to interact.

How to Play

  • Catch clean drops to fill your jerry can (10 max).
  • Avoid polluted drops. They cost a heart and 20 points.
  • Use the biosand filter on the bridge to turn your can into clean water.
  • Pick up litter for a cleanup bonus.
  • Stay out of the Restricted Area. The guard sends you back and you lose 25 points.
  • Fill the village tank to 100 to level up. Clear level 5 to win.
  • Grab cases at the end of the conveyor belt (carry up to 3).
  • Load the truck before its timer runs out.
  • Leaking cases go in the recycling bin. Loading one costs 30 points.
  • Jump over the forklift. A hit costs 25 points and drops your cases.
  • Spills slow you down. Interact to mop them up for a bonus.
  • Each full truck heads to the villages. Send 5 trucks to win.
